            How XP works

            A finished task is worth 10 XP, a habit 5, each minute of focus 1, a paper sat 100 and a deed 50. Each of the day's three quests adds 30, and all three add 40 more. Earn 150 XP in a day to keep your streak going. Undoing something takes its XP back.

            Your data

            Daybook has no accounts and no server. Your tasks, habits, exams and notes are kept in this browser's storage and never sent anywhere. The only way they leave is a link you copy yourself, so treat that link like the notes in it.

            Two outside services are used. Open-Meteo gets your place's coordinates to send back the forecast, and the town you type when changing place. Google Fonts serves the lettering. Neither one sees your Daybook.

            Clearing this browser's site data erases your Daybook. Copy a link first if you want a backup.
